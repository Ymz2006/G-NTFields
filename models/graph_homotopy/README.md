# `models/graph_homotopy`: one cone, one ridge, on the double cover (2026-10-17)

The branch decoder (`models/graph_branch`) reduced to what it is about: the goal cone and one ridge, with the ridge
put where the topology puts it. Head: `models/graph_method_triangle/homotopy_decoder.py` (arch `graph_homotopy`);
trainer: this package (graph_lie pipeline, original metric NTFields procedure, Lie gradient, isotropic clearance
speed); cover planner: `scripts/plan_eval_homotopy.py`; log: `EXPERIMENTS.md`; results: `results/graph_homotopy/`.

## 1. Formulation

**The cover.** SE(3) = R^3 x SO(3) has the double cover R^3 x S^3 (unit quaternions, q and -q distinct points,
R(q) = R(-q)). pi_1(SO(3)) = Z_2: between two poses there are exactly two homotopy classes of paths, and a path from x
to y lifts, from a lift x~ of x, to a path ending either at y~ or at -y~. The two classes are the two lifts of the
goal. The travel-time field is therefore first defined on the cover,

    T~ : (R^3 x S^3) x (R^3 x S^3) -> R,      T~(x~, y~) = time of the fastest path from x to y in the class of y~,

and the time between poses is the minimum over the two classes, the only minimum of the decoder:

    T(x, y) = min( T~(x~, y~), T~(x~, -y~) ).

The cover is simply connected, so T~(., y~) is the Eikonal solution with the lifted speed S~(x~) = S(x) and has no
rotational ridge of its own; the ridge of T is exactly where the two classes tie (the half turn in free space,
moved by obstacles). The branch decoder's K residual branches per route are gone: no extra minima.

**Deck invariance, built in.** Flipping both quaternions is the same pair of poses, so T~(-x~, -y~) = T~(x~, y~)
must hold; then the set {T~(x~, +-y~)} does not depend on which lifts are picked and T is a function on
SE(3) x SE(3). The head reads only invariants of the diagonal Z_2 action: the even per-pose embedding G(x) = (t, vec
R) through the G-Shape encoder e(x); the lifted relative rotation q_x^* q_y = (c, v) (c = q_x . q_y; invariant under
the simultaneous flip, negated by a single flip, which is the sheet swap); and the q-odd per-pose coordinates
Q(x~) = sum_i q_i W_i e(x) compared through |Q(x~) - Q(y~)|^2, a quadratic form in q_x (x) q_y. (The polynomial
invariants of the diagonal action are generated by the quadratic monomials in (q_x, q_y), i.e. by (R_x, R_y, q_x
q_y^T); the head is built from these.)

**The cover field.** Per sheet, every term >= 0 and symmetric:

    T~ = T_free (1 + P) + C + delta
    T_free = sqrt(|t_x - t_y|^2 / s_t^2 + psi^2 / s_r^2),   psi = 2 atan2(|v|, c) in [0, 2 pi]      the cone
    C      = rho_bar lam_bar tanh(T_free / lam_bar)          conformal scale 1 + rho(p) of the cone (per-pose heads)
    delta  = |E(x) - E(y)|^2 + |Q(x~) - Q(y~)|^2             smooth deviation on the cover
    P      = softplus(h), h symmetrised MLP on (p(e_x), p(e_y), c, v, T_free)     optional pair term (pair_dim > 0)

Properties for every parameter value (`tests/test_graph_homotopy.py`): T >= T_free >= 0 and T(x, x) = 0 exactly;
symmetry; T = sqrt(Q) with Q smooth to second order at the goal and the cone = the metric's shape scaled by
1 + rho(p) + P(p, p); peaks only (sqrt at the goal, the far point of a sheet, one min); the ridge moves with the
learned terms; no cap; deck invariance exact; value = min of the sheets for any lifts.

**Training on the cover.** The Eikonal residual is formed on lifted pairs (`model_function_homotopy.py`): `lift min`
trains the sheet that is the minimum (the base field; bit-identical to the conformal branch head with K = 1),
`lift random` / `both` train both sheets everywhere (T~ = the Eikonal solution on the whole cover).

**Planning on the cover.** `scripts/plan_eval_homotopy.py`: the official forward MPPI with the costs taken from
T~(x~, sigma y~), the pose of every sample lifted continuously from the unwrapped rotation-vector chart (q = exp(pi
r), a full turn arrives at -q). `cover`: one plan towards the lift the field prefers at the start (sigma* = argmin);
`dual`: both lifted goals are planned (the two homotopy classes) and the converged plan is kept (SE(3) convergence,
then chart convergence, then fewer iterations; `success_se3_shorter` records the converged-then-shorter-path rule).
`either` (at least one sheet plan succeeded) is the oracle bound for any selection rule.

## 2. Results (EXPERIMENTS.md H1-H10; tables: results/graph_homotopy/summary/plan_summary.md)

Pipeline: models/graph_lie trainer (original metric NTFields procedure: AdamW wd 0.5, lr 5e-4, 5 x 2000 pairs per
epoch, accept / reject, `exp(-tau/2)`, `1e-2 (sqrt(N) - 1)^2`), Lie gradient, isotropic clearance speed, one RTX 5070
(5 runs share it, 25 min per 4000 epochs). Evaluation: per-case forward MPPI (50 samples, horizon 5, step 0.015,
temperature 50, 200 iterations), 1000 RRT-verified cases per standard set, batch 125, seed 0; SE(3) success (converged
to within 0.01 in the product metric, no collision along the way) is the primary metric. The big-angle /
rotation-heavy T sets of the earlier campaigns are not on this machine.

* **Planning on the cover is the gain, and it is the ridge.** The same field planned by the official planner on the
  base field and by the cover planner (both lifted goals, converged plan kept) differs by +1.9 (T), +1.0 (L) and
  +0.5 (A, +0.7 with the shorter-path rule) points over 5 seeds each; paired per case (5000 per scene), the cover
  planner recovers 109 base failures and loses 12 on T (exact McNemar p = 1e-20), +62 / -13 on L (p = 8e-9) and
  +59 / -22 on A with the shorter-path rule (p = 5e-5). By relative rotation the
  base planner reaches 94.1 / 95.8 / 96.4 % (T / L / A) at 150-180 deg and the cover planner 99.0 / 98.9 / 99.0 %;
  below 120 deg both are at 97-99 %
  (`results/graph_homotopy/summary/angle_*.md`). The field's own choice of class at the start (sigma*, one plan) is
  not the gain (+22 / -15, p = 0.32): where the field says "the long way" (15 % of the cases, 35 % of those near the
  half turn) the long-way plan succeeds less often than the short one. Planning both classes and keeping the
  converged plan (ties: fewer iterations, or the shorter path) is within 0.1-0.4 points of the oracle that knows
  which sheet plan succeeded.
* **The reduced head is as good as the branch decoder it replaces.** With the branch decoder's schedule (4000 epochs)
  the plain head reaches 95.5 +- 0.6 (T), 97.1 +- 0.3 (L), 97.4 +- 0.7 (A) with the base planner; the conformal branch
  head K = 4 of the earlier campaign had 95.4 +- 0.6 / 97.5 +- 0.4 / 97.9 +- 0.7 (3 RTX 3090, same code), and retrained
  here with the same schedule it reaches 94.7 +- 0.4 (base planner; it has no cover field to plan on). The extra residual branches were not where the gain was.
* **Training length.** The Eikonal loss is still falling at epoch 4000; 8000 epochs take the base-planner success from
  95.5 to 97.0 (T), 97.1 to 97.9 (L), 97.4 to 98.4 (A), collisions from 2.2 % to 0.4 % on T. 16000 epochs give no more (T seed 0: 97.1 base, 98.6 dual, loss 1.50e-3 vs 1.49e-3 at 8000): the gain saturates at 8000.
* **Training the far sheet.** Lifting every training pair to a uniformly random sheet (`lift random`, the cover
  field everywhere) or to both sheets costs 3-4 points of base-planner success on T (91.1-91.3 vs 94.6 at seed 0):
  the far sheet competes for the same capacity, the speed calibration in the slow decades worsens (dex 0.58-0.60 vs
  0.54), and sigma* does not become reliable. A light lift (10 % of the pairs on the far sheet, `hrand01`) keeps the
  base field (95.0) and gave the best seed-0 dual result (98.0 vs 97.1); at 8000 epochs it matches the plain head (96.4 / 98.6 vs 96.7 / 98.5 at seed 0), so the two do not stack. With the min
  lift the far sheet is trained only where it is the minimum, yet its Eikonal residual (median 0.020) is close to the
  near sheet's (0.017): T_free + smooth terms keep the untrained sheet sane, which is what the dual planner relies on.
* **Head variants.** 32 + 32 residual coordinates: +1.0 base at seed 0 (4000 epochs), but at 8000 epochs over 3 seeds
  97.0 +- 0.4 base / 99.0 +- 0.2 dual, level with the plain head (97.0 / 98.9); 64 + 64: -1.7 at seed 0. The invariant pair term P helps
  when it starts small (init 0.01: 96.2 base, 1.4 % collisions at seed 0; 3 seeds on T 95.4 +- 0.8 base / 97.5 +- 0.5 dual, a wash against the plain head) and not at 0.05.
* **Properties on trained weights** (`scripts/field_diag.py`, `scripts/ridge_slice.py`): T(p, p) = 0 and asymmetry
  <= 1e-6 on every run; min T / T_free 1.04-1.14 (the lower bound holds with margin); the field prefers the long way
  on 2-7 % of random pairs; every rotation slice through a test case crosses exactly one ridge, 3-11 deg from the
  free-space half-turn position for the 8000-epoch field, further where an obstacle makes one class slower
  (`results/graph_homotopy/figures/T_hminL_s0/`).

    --dataPath testing_data/3dshape/Tshape3d_env1 --out results/graph_homotopy/plan/T_hmin_s0/Tshape3d_env1__dual.json   # cwd = Lie repo
